---
title: Payment Links
description: The difference between a reusable link and an invoice, and why there is no subscription.
---

A Payment Link is the unit you share with a customer. There are two kinds, and
the choice decides how many times it can be paid.

## Reusable link

One URL, unlimited payments, any number of payers. It stays open until you
revoke it or it expires.

Every payment through it produces its own **reference**, so fifty people paying
the same link produce fifty distinguishable records. The link's own `ref` is
shared by all of them — use the per-payment reference when you need to tell
payments apart.

## Invoice

Payable exactly once. As soon as a payment is recorded the invoice closes:
checkout tells the next visitor it has already been paid, and the API refuses a
second payment with `409`.

A *failed* attempt does not close it. Only a payment that was received —
verified, shielded, or awaiting shielding — counts.

## Expiry and revocation

Both kinds can carry an expiry, and both can be revoked from the link's own page
at any time. Revoking is the way to kill a link you have already shared.

## Why there is no subscription

Recurring billing means pulling funds on a schedule against a standing mandate.
A shielded balance cannot be debited without its holder signing, so there is no
mandate to give. This is a limit of the privacy model, not a missing feature.
