---
title: Quickstart
description: From connecting a wallet to a paid link, and where to look when it lands.
---

## 1. Connect the wallet you want paid into

Open the console and connect. That wallet **is** your login — there is no
signup, and no password. Everything you create is scoped to it, and separately
to the network you are on: test-mode links never appear in live mode.

## 2. Create a link

Go to **Payment Links → Create payment link** and pick what you're making:

- **Reusable link** — one URL, any number of payers. Use it for donations, a
  product, a tip jar.
- **Invoice** — payable once. After the first payment it closes, and anyone
  opening it afterwards is told it has already been paid.

Set a token and either a fixed amount or let the customer enter one. Under
**advanced options** you can set a redirect so the payer returns to your site
afterwards.

## 3. Share it, and let them pay

The customer opens the link and chooses:

- **Pay privately** — a shielded STRK20 transfer. Needs a privacy-capable
  wallet (Ready or Xverse).
- **Pay with any wallet** — an ordinary public transfer. Works with any
  Starknet wallet; the payment is public, but which business received it is not.

## 4. Watch it land

The payment appears under **Transactions** with its own reference. Click any row
for the full record: amount, status, flow, the on-chain transaction, and the
link it came from.

<Callout title="Verify before you deliver value">
If you are integrating from your own backend, do not treat the redirect as
proof. Call the [verify endpoint](/docs/api#verify-a-payment) with the payment's
reference and check both the status **and** the amount.
</Callout>

## 5. Cash out

**Payouts** withdraws from your balance to any address, either publicly
(unshielded, use this to cash out) or privately (stays shielded — the
destination needs its own privacy-capable wallet already registered on STRK20).
