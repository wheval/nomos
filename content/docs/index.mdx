---
title: What Nomos is
description: A private payment gateway for Starknet — and a straight account of what it hides, what it doesn't, and who has to be trusted.
---

Nomos lets a business accept payments on Starknet without its revenue or its
customers' payments appearing on the public chain. You create a Payment Link,
share it, and the customer pays with their wallet. Settlement clears against
the live STRK20 privacy pool.

## What is actually private

<Callout type="warn" title="Read this before you build on it">
Privacy here is **from the public chain and from third parties**. It is not
privacy from Nomos. Nomos is custodial, and the section below says exactly what
that means.
</Callout>

| | Hidden on-chain | Visible |
|---|---|---|
| Pay privately (Flow A) | Sender, recipient, amount | That *a* private transaction happened |
| Pay with any wallet (Flow B) | Which business was paid | Payer, amount, that a transfer occurred |
| Merchant balance | Held in Nomos's ledger, never on-chain | — |
| Payout | Optional: settle privately or publicly | — |

Both flows settle into **one operating wallet** shared by every merchant. A
merchant's balance is a claim in Nomos's ledger, not an on-chain account they
hold. They cash out with a payout whenever they choose.

## The trust you are taking on

This is a deliberate trade, not an oversight. An ordinary wallet's payment has
to land somewhere before it can be shielded, so a purely non-custodial router
could not serve those customers at all.

What follows from it:

- **Nomos can link a payer to a merchant.** For private payments, the customer's
  transaction is submitted from their own wallet, so its sender is public.
  Nomos stores that transaction hash next to the merchant and the amount.
  Anyone with database access could join the two — which is precisely what the
  chain hides from everyone else.
- **Funds sit in shared custody** between payment and payout.
- **The operating wallet signs with a software key today.** An explicit
  stand-in for real key-management infrastructure, not the intended end state.

## What Nomos does not do

Subscriptions, direct debit, recurring charges, pre-authorisation and partial
debits are all absent, and not because they are unbuilt. Each needs to *pull*
funds later against a standing mandate, and a shielded balance cannot be
debited without its holder signing every time. That is a property of the
privacy model.
