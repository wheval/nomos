---
title: Limits and status
description: What is shipped, what is partial, and what is deliberately absent — kept honest rather than aspirational.
---

## Shipped

- Payment Links, both reusable and single-use invoices
- Both payment flows, each verified on-chain before a merchant is credited
- Per-payment references, a verify endpoint, signed webhooks with retries
- Payouts, public or private
- Test and live separation, down to separate API keys and separate balances

## Partial

**Shielding a public payment is manual.** Moving a Flow B payment into the pool
needs an FPI screening signature that cannot be produced headlessly, so it is a
team-operated step. Until it completes, the payment sits at `pending_shield`
and the merchant is not credited.

**Mainnet is unproven.** The token addresses are verified against the official
registry and the pool demonstrably holds USDC, but no Nomos payment has run
through mainnet — and cannot until a mainnet proving service is available.
Payouts refuse outright on any network other than Sepolia rather than
half-work.

**Webhook retries are in-process.** They do not survive a restart. Reconcile
against the verify endpoint.

## Not built, and why

Subscriptions, direct debit, recurring charges, pre-authorisation, partial
debits. All of them pull funds later against a standing mandate, and a shielded
balance cannot be debited without its holder signing each time.

Refunds and disputes are possible but need a payout-back path. Split payments
need multi-recipient settlement. Neither is in scope yet.

## Known operational issues

**Ready X: "enable private tokens" works once per seed phrase.** Reported by
other teams during the hackathon — the key appears to be derived from the seed
rather than per account, so enabling on a second account in the same wallet
fails. The only workaround found was reinstalling the wallet. If a customer
reports a payment failing despite holding a balance, this is the first thing to
check.

**The operating wallet signs with a software key.** An explicit stand-in for
real key-management infrastructure.
