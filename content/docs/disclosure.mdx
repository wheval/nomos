title: Disclosure
description: Proving what you were paid — what Nomos can show, what it cannot, and who has to be trusted for each.
---

Privacy that cannot be undone on demand is not much use to a business. At some
point an accountant, a tax authority, an investor or a bank asks you to show
what you took in, and "it's private" is not an answer any of them accept.

This page is about that moment: what Nomos can prove, and what it cannot.

## Export your record

**Transactions → Export CSV** downloads every payment recorded against your
wallet on the network you are on. Each row carries the date, your own
reference, the note, the gross amount, the Nomos fee and the net — so the
arithmetic closes against your balance rather than needing to be recomputed.

Amounts are exact decimal strings, never rounded, because the attribution
scheme depends on differences of a millionth of a unit.

You can also read the same data as JSON from
[`GET /api/payments`](/docs/api#balances-and-payments) and shape it yourself.

## What that export is, honestly

It is **your record, attested by Nomos**. That is the same thing a Stripe or
Paystack export is, and it is enough for the situations above, because those
parties already accept a processor's statement.

It is **not a cryptographic proof**. A reader who does not trust Nomos cannot
verify it against the chain, because the payments it describes are shielded —
that is the entire point of them. Anyone telling you a privacy system gives
you both properties at once is selling one of them short.

## What Nomos cannot do

**Nomos cannot grant someone a view of your payments alone.** The privacy pool
has a viewing key per account, and the account receiving payments is Nomos's
operating wallet — one wallet, shared by every merchant. Its viewing key
reveals every merchant's payments at once. There is no per-merchant viewing
key to hand out, so there is nothing to selectively disclose.

This falls out of the custody model described in
[Privacy](/docs/privacy#custody-stated-plainly): Nomos holds funds between the
payment and your payout, so payments arrive in its wallet, not yours.
Non-custodial settlement would change this, and would make a real viewing-key
grant possible. It is on the roadmap and is not what is offered today.

## What is actually private

Worth restating precisely, because "private" is doing specific work here:

| | Can see your payments |
| --- | --- |
| The public chain | **No** — amounts, senders and your total volume are not public |
| Anyone watching your address | **No** |
| Nomos | **Yes** — it has to, in order to settle them |
| Anyone you export to | **Yes**, and only what you export |

The privacy that Nomos delivers is privacy **from the chain**. That is the
property a business actually lacks when it accepts stablecoins today, and it
is the one this product exists to restore. Privacy from Nomos is a different
property, it requires non-custodial settlement, and Nomos does not claim it.
