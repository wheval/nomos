title: Who sees what
description: Every party that learns something when a payment happens, including the ones the customer did not choose.

## The public chain

| Flow | What an observer learns |
|---|---|
| A (private) | That a shielded transaction occurred. Not who, not whom, not how much. |
| B (public) | The payer, the amount, and that the operating wallet was paid. Not which business. |

Merchant balances never appear on-chain at all — they live in Nomos's ledger
until a payout.

## Nomos

Nomos sees everything: which merchant, which amount, which token, when, and —
for Flow A — the customer's own transaction hash, whose sender is public.

**Storing that hash is what links a payer to a merchant.** It is necessary
(it's how a payment is deduplicated and verified), and it is the single
largest privacy liability in the design. Anyone with database access can take
that hash, look up its sender on-chain, and learn that this customer paid this
merchant this amount.

## The payer's wallet

Ready or Xverse generate the proof for a private payment client-side. Nomos
never sees the customer's keys.

## The prover

Private payouts from the operating wallet are proved through a proving service.
For the hackathon this is StarkWare-operated infrastructure; a production
deployment is expected to run its own.

## The auditor question

STRK20 pool registration has its own disclosure properties. Nomos does not
change them, and does not claim to — read the protocol's own documentation
before assuming what registration does and does not reveal.
