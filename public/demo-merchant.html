A small, ordinary storefront — this page has nothing to do with Nomos or Starknet beyond one script tag below. That's the point: a business drops in a button, a customer pays privately, and the merchant never had to build anything on-chain themselves.

This is a demo storefront proving the embed, not a real product or business. The recipient address above is a placeholder, not a live merchant account. Integration is one <script> tag with data-nomos-to, data-nomos-amount, and data-nomos-note — see /widget.js for the full attribute list (adds data-nomos-exp for an expiring request, and data-nomos-ref for a reconciliation reference).
